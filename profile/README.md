**XWiki Contrib** is a community dedicated to collaborative development of [XWiki](https://www.xwiki.org) related projects and extensions, in the spirit of wiki communities.

These projects are not part of the official XWiki distributions and are not maintained by the XWiki core development team: each one has its own maintainers. Projects without an active maintainer are moved to the [xwiki-attic](https://github.com/xwiki-attic) organization.

Find out how to contribute to a project, or request a new one, on [contrib.xwiki.org](https://contrib.xwiki.org). The extensions themselves are documented and installable from [extensions.xwiki.org](https://extensions.xwiki.org).
